### Business-Sales-Report
###### Retail Sales Analysis Report highlights

#### About This Project
##### DATA SUMMARY:
The Retail Sales Analysis Report highlights the overall performance of the business across sales, profit, regions, products, sales representatives, customers, and payment methods. The business generated $70.33 million in revenue, $6.49 million in profit, and sold 25,355 units. Sales performance remained relatively stable throughout the year, with strong contributions from the Northern region, the Furniture product category, and top-performing sales representatives.

##### OBJECTIVES: 
* Analyze overall business sales and profitability.
* Identify the highest and lowest performing regions.
* Evaluate product category profitability.
* Assess sales representative performance.
* Understand customer distribution and purchasing channels.
* Review customer payment preferences to support business decisions.

##### KEY FINDINGS:
##### Sales Performance.
* Total Revenue: $70,329,940.71
* Total Profit: $6,487,847.07
* Total Quantity Sold: 25,355 units

##### Regional Performance:
* North Region generated the highest sales (1,661,461).
* South Region recorded the lowest sales (1,519,737).

##### Product Category Performance.
* Furniture achieved the highest profit percentage (37.90%).
* Food generated the lowest profit percentage (32.68%).

#### Sales Representative Performance.
* Eve was the top-performing sales representative with 6,705 sales.
* Alice followed closely with 6,486 sales.
* David recorded the lowest sales performance (5,808 sales).

#### Customer & Sales Channel Analysis.
* Total Customers: 1,000
* Sales channels were almost evenly distributed:
  
   * Retail: 512 customers
   * Online: 488 customers
    
#### Payment Method Analysis
* Credit Card: 34.5%
* Bank Transfer: 34.2%
* Cash: 31.3%

##### RECOMMENDATIONS:
* The Food category should be removed from the market because it generates less profit compared to other products.
* The Southern Region should offer discounts to attract more customers and improve sales performance.
* Eve should be transferred to the Southern Region to help attract more customers and increase sales.

#### APPROACH:
* For this analysis, I examined the retail sales data to evaluate the overall performance of the business. I analyzed key metrics such as revenue, profit, profit percentage, and quantity sold, while also exploring performance across regions, product categories, sales representatives, customers, sales channels, and payment methods.
* I used the dashboard's visualizations, including KPI cards, bar charts, and donut charts, to identify trends, compare performance, and uncover meaningful insights. My focus was on identifying the strongest and weakest performing areas of the business, assessing profitability, evaluating sales representative performance, and understanding customer purchasing patterns.
* By analyzing these key business metrics, I was able to draw data-driven conclusions and provide recommendations aimed at improving sales performance, profitability, and overall business growth.

###### LIMITATIONS:
* The dashboard presents aggregated results, making it difficult to identify the specific factors        driving sales and profit performance.
* Customer analysis is limited to customer counts and sales channels; deeper insights into customer      behavior and preferences are not available.
* Product analysis focuses primarily on profit percentage and does not provide detailed information on   product costs, pricing, or revenue contribution by category.
* The dashboard captures performance within a single reporting period and does not provide historical    comparisons to evaluate long-term growth trends.
* Regional analysis highlights performance differences but does not explain the underlying causes of     stronger or weaker sales across regions.
* The report focuses on sales performance metrics and does not include operational, inventory, or        supply chain data that may affect business outcomes.
